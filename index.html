Personal OS — Mail Sync

A private, single-user tool that files the owner's own email into their own notes system.

What it does

Personal OS is a personal knowledge system built on plain text files. This component reads the owner's own Gmail messages and saves them as notes in a private repository, so they can be searched alongside the owner's other records.

Who it's for

One person — the owner of the accounts it reads. It is not a product, is not offered to other users, has no sign-up, and no commercial purpose.

What it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly). It never sends, deletes, modifies or labels mail.
  • Only the accounts the owner has personally authorised.

Where the data goes

Into a private repository controlled by the owner. Nothing is shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising or model training.
